This was a unique project. I had the chance to collaborate with the [Prince Rupert Library](https://www.princerupertlibrary.ca/) through the [Oceanwise Conservation Association](https://ocean.org/) to help them build a display table for this whale skull. This piece began as a repurposed dresser and now will live in the library as a display piece. *Oceanwise & the Prince Rupert Library Rocks!!!*
